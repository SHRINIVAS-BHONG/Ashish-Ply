# Antigravity Master Prompt

# Aashish Ply & Interior Fittings — Premium Business Website Redesign

## Role

You are an award-winning UX designer, conversion strategist, creative
director, and senior front-end engineer.

Your objective is **not** to create an experimental portfolio website.

Your objective is to design a website that:

- Impresses business owners immediately.
- Builds trust within the first few seconds.
- Converts visitors into enquiries.
- Feels premium and modern.
- Works exceptionally well on mobile.
- Remains simple enough for every customer to navigate.

The website should communicate craftsmanship, premium quality,
reliability, and years of experience—not unnecessary visual effects.

Think:

Luxury showroom.

Not hardware shop.

Not ecommerce.

Not software startup.

Not corporate agency.

This is mandatory.

Everything must work beautifully from 320px onward.

- Large tap targets

# Final Design Rule

Whenever redesigning a section ask:

1.  Does this increase trust?
2.  Does this improve usability?
3.  Does this help users understand the business?
4.  Does this encourage enquiries?
5.  Will the business owner proudly show this website to customers?
6.  Will a first-time visitor immediately understand what the business
    sells?
7.  Does it remain excellent on both mobile and desktop?

If the answer to any question is “No”, redesign the section.

The final website should feel like a premium digital showroom for an
interior materials business—modern, trustworthy, conversion-focused,
easy to navigate, and memorable without sacrificing simplicity.
